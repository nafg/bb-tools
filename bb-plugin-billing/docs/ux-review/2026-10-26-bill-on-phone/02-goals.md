# Goals: what a user wants from the Billing page, in their words

Use case: "On my phone, bill an archived client thread's unbilled work to Zoho
Books and be sure I did not bill anything twice."

The owner's own questions from the session of 2026-10-26 are findings in their
own right (ux-roast §5); each is listed under the goal it belongs to, marked
**Asked**.

- **G1. See at a glance what is waiting for me.** "Which threads still need
  billing, how much, and for whom." Then open one and get on with it.
  Asked: "When I click review and bill, it's not clear anything happened."
- **G2. Know what each stretch of work was, well enough to decide whether to
  bill it.** "What was I doing from 02:40 to 03:41? Is this billable?"
  Asked: "I can't figure out what I'm looking at … it's only showing me the
  messages that I sent, not what the model did … very hard to tell what
  exactly I was working on." Asked: "could the messages link to where they
  are in the actual thread?"
- **G3. Pick exactly which stretches go into this submission.** Leave some
  out, bill only up to a moment, include the subthreads or not.
  Asked: "up to here doesn't look like a button."
- **G4. Settle shared time.** When two threads were open at once, say who gets
  it, and know what the other thread was.
  Asked: "how am I supposed to know what that thread is" (a bare thread id).
- **G5. Avoid billing time that is already in Zoho by hand.** The agent ran
  the Zoho timer in some threads; the timer may have been paused.
  Asked: "Didn't I put in the prompt to infer from the MCP calls? … pre
  existing entries … and determine if I already double billed." Asked:
  "When was the timer started and when was it stopped?" Asked: "It shouldn't
  correspond to 3 hours on the clock. What if the timer was paused?"
- **G6. Read, fix and approve the notes that go to the client.** Per day:
  what the entry will say; make it accurate.
  Asked: "Did you ever implement summarizing the summaries?" "The first
  summary should be longer so that the second summary can be accurate."
- **G7. Send, and know for certain what was sent.** What went, what is left,
  and that nothing happened twice.
  Asked: "the Send button … stayed there; it didn't close like I sent it."
- **G8. Undo a send without losing anything else.**
  Asked: "will it lose the activity after it?" "I submitted activity later
  than it as well." "I did undo on the 3h 21m but it shows already submitted
  only on one 28m span."
- **G9. Do all of this on a phone**, one hand, with the text readable and the
  controls tappable, without horizontal scrolling or guessing what a column
  means. Asked: "Still terrible UX, especially on mobile."
- **G10. Understand the state of a thread and of the page at any moment**:
  what is billed, what is not, what the page is doing right now.
  Asked: "Terrible UX and seems buggy too, unclear what happened or my state."
  "Like I said, state machines."
- **G11. Not pay for or wait on model runs I did not ask for**, and know when
  one is running. Asked: "I hope it's not recalculating the summary every
  time."
